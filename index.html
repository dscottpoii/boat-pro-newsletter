?? Pre-Winterization Preparation

?? Important Timing

Begin winterization before the first hard freeze in your area. In most regions, this means starting in October or early November.

Essential Supplies Checklist

Engine Supplies
  • Marine antifreeze (non-toxic)
  • Engine oil & filter
  • Gear oil
  • Fogging oil spray
Cleaning Supplies
  • Boat soap
  • Hull cleaner
  • Wax or sealant
  • Mildew preventative
Protection Items
  • Moisture absorbers
  • Boat cover or shrink wrap
  • Support stands
  • Battery tender
Tools Needed
  • Basic hand tools
  • Oil pump
  • Antifreeze pump kit
  • Multimeter

?? Engine Winterization Process

?? Boat Pro Tip

Run the engine up to operating temperature before starting the winterization process. This ensures contaminants are suspended in the oil for better removal.

Step-by-Step Engine Protection

1

Change Engine Oil & Filter

Warm the engine, then change oil and filter while warm. Old oil contains acids and moisture that can damage engine components over winter.

2

Flush Cooling System

For raw water-cooled engines, flush with fresh water for 10 minutes. For closed cooling systems, check antifreeze strength (-50°F protection minimum).

3

Add Antifreeze to Raw Water System

Run non-toxic marine antifreeze through the raw water side until pink antifreeze exits the exhaust.

4

Fog the Engine

Spray fogging oil into the carburetor or throttle body while engine is running, then into each spark plug hole.

5

Service Lower Unit

Change gear oil in the lower unit. Check for water contamination (milky appearance). Replace if necessary.

? Engine Winterization Checklist

  • Engine oil and filter changed
  • Transmission fluid checked/changed
  • Coolant system protected to -50°F
  • Raw water system filled with antifreeze
  • Engine fogged internally
  • Air filter cleaned or replaced
  • Belts and hoses inspected
  • Spark plugs removed and coated

? Fuel System Protection

?? Ethanol Alert

E10 gasoline can absorb water and cause phase separation. Proper fuel treatment is essential for winter storage.

Fuel Tank Management

1

Fill Tank to 95% Capacity

A full tank minimizes condensation. Leave 5% space for expansion.

2

Add Fuel Stabilizer

Use marine-grade stabilizer at recommended ratio. Run engine for 10 minutes to circulate.

3

Change Fuel Filters

Replace all fuel filters and water separators. Fill new filters with clean fuel before installation.

?? Water & Plumbing Systems

Fresh Water System

? Fresh Water Winterization

  • Drain water heater completely
  • Bypass water heater if equipped
  • Pump antifreeze through all faucets
  • Run antifreeze through shower systems
  • Fill all P-traps with antifreeze
  • Pour antifreeze in shower sump
  • Winterize deck washdown systems

Head System

?? Boat Pro Tip

Pump the head dry, then add antifreeze and pump through entire system. Don't forget the holding tank vent line!

? Head System Checklist

  • Pump out holding tank
  • Flush with fresh water
  • Add antifreeze to bowl
  • Pump antifreeze through system
  • Close all seacocks
  • Label closed seacocks

?? Electrical System Maintenance

Battery Care

1

Remove and Clean Batteries

Clean terminals with baking soda solution. Check specific gravity if applicable.

2

Charge Fully

Bring batteries to full charge. Store in cool, dry location on wood blocks.

3

Maintain During Storage

Use battery tender or check monthly. Recharge as needed to prevent sulfation.

? Electrical System Checklist

  • All electronics disconnected or protected
  • Antennas lowered and secured
  • Shore power disconnected
  • Bilge pump tested and clear
  • All switches turned off
  • Corrosion preventive applied to connections

?? Hull & Exterior Protection

Cleaning & Protection

1

Thorough Cleaning

Wash hull, deck, and topsides. Remove all salt, dirt, and stains. Clean and treat any mildew.

2

Apply Protective Coating

Wax fiberglass surfaces. Apply UV protectant to vinyl and rubber. Treat teak as needed.

3

Inspect & Repair

Check for cracks, chips, or damage. Make repairs before storage to prevent water intrusion.

?? Canvas & Cover Care

Ensure all canvas is completely dry before storage. Support cover properly to prevent water pooling and provide adequate ventilation.

?? Interior Winterization

Moisture Prevention

? Interior Preparation Checklist

  • Remove all food and perishables
  • Clean refrigerator and prop door open
  • Remove electronics and valuables
  • Clean all surfaces with mildew preventative
  • Remove or wash all fabric items
  • Place moisture absorbers throughout
  • Open all lockers and doors for ventilation
  • Vacuum and clean bilges thoroughly

?? Boat Pro Tip

Leave cabinet doors and drawers slightly open to promote air circulation. Use cedar blocks or lavender sachets for natural odor control.

??? Storage Preparation

Storage Options

Indoor Storage

Best protection from elements. Control temperature and humidity. Higher cost but maximum protection.

Shrink Wrap

Good weather protection. Ensure proper ventilation. Check periodically for damage.

Canvas Cover

Reusable option. Must fit properly. Requires frame support. Good ventilation essential.

Outdoor Uncovered

Least expensive. Requires most preparation. Higher risk of damage. More spring work needed.

Final Storage Checklist

? Ready for Storage

  • Boat properly supported on stands
  • Weight distributed evenly
  • Drain plug removed (if stored outside)
  • Cover installed and ventilated
  • Insurance notified of storage location
  • Registration and documents removed
  • Inventory of removed items created
  • Spring commissioning list started

?? Spring Commissioning Preview

Document everything you do during winterization to make spring commissioning easier:

? Spring Commissioning Notes

  • List all fluids that need replacing
  • Note all parts removed or disconnected
  • Mark calendar for service intervals
  • Schedule professional services early
  • Order parts and supplies in advance
  • Plan launch date and reserve slip/mooring
