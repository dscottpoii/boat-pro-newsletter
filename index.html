The Ultimate Boat Winterization Guide

Freeze damage to an engine block or plumbing system is catastrophic and expensive. Here's the field-tested rundown.

  1. Engine & fuel system: warm the engine, circulate antifreeze until it runs pink at the exhaust, fog the cylinders.
  2. Plumbing: bypass the water heater, pump antifreeze through every line until it flows clean at each faucet.
  3. Interior & exterior: strip fabrics, wax the hull, control moisture with absorbers.
  4. Battery & electrical: pull batteries, store on a smart maintainer — a discharged battery can freeze and crack.
  5. Cover & storage: shrink-wrap or canvas, supported and ventilated to stop mildew before it starts.
